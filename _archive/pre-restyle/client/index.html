THE SHORT VERSION

Remember less.
Win more.

Four secret cards. Look at two, once. The lowest total wins.

  1. Draw or call. Take the deck top privately, or the public discard. Calling uses your turn and gives your friend one final turn.
  2. Keep or discard. Replace one card, or several matching own cards. A public discard must be kept face up. Deck cards stay secret.
  3. Use a little mischief. Discard a deck-drawn 7/8 to peek at yours, 9/10 to spy at theirs, 11/12 to swap one each without looking.
7–8 PEEK9–10 SPY11–12 SWAP

A failed multi-card match exposes your selected cards and adds the drawn card; three or more also adds one unseen penalty card. Caller lowest/tied: 0 points. Otherwise: sum +10. Everyone else scores their sum. Exactly 12,12,13,13: you score 0, they score 50.

Quick: four rounds. Classic: first total above 100 ends the match; exactly 100 resets to 50 once. Deck exhaustion also ends a round. No snap/slap rule. Original prototype, not affiliated with CABO publishers or Nintendo.
