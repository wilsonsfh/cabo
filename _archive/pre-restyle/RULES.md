# Chosen rules: CABO second edition, two players

Canonical source is the Bezier-attributed 2019 second-edition rule sheet, opened at https://board-game-rules.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/cabo_Official-Rules.pdf. The analyst independently reconciled it against https://whatsericplaying.com/2019/03/25/cabo/ and https://cardrulesplus.com/games/cabo/. I additionally read https://treyhunner.com/2015/06/cabo-card-game/ and https://fathergeek.com/cabo/; those describe older/different versions and do not override the canonical sheet. Deluxe publisher PDF mirror and https://beziergames.com/pages/cabo confirm the scoring and four-round option.

The explicit deck manifest used here has 52 cards (the opened 2019 sheet does not enumerate multiplicities): two 0s, four each of ranks 1–12, two 13s. Four cards per player, fixed numbered slots. Secretly inspect exactly two, once; explicitly hide them to start. Start player chosen by seeded RNG. On later rounds the lowest previous-round score starts, ties resolved clockwise from prior starter.

On your turn draw privately from the deck, take the public discard top, or call. A deck card may replace one card, replace multiple matching cards of your own, be discarded, or be discarded to use its ability. A discard card MUST replace and remains face up. Deck replacements remain face down. Exchanged cards enter the discard face up. Successful multiple exchange removes all selected cards and puts the drawn card in the first selected slot; empty slots remain to preserve spatial memory.

If selected cards do not all match, return them face up and add the drawn card at the line's end. For three or four selected cards, also add an unseen deck penalty card. The drawn card keeps its source orientation: face down from deck, face up from discard. If the last deck card was drawn, resolve the full action before scoring; no additional penalty can be taken from an already empty deck. This exhaustion edge is a documented implementation choice because the sheet does not specify it.

7/8: peek at one own face-down card. 9/10: spy at one opponent face-down card. 11/12: swap one own and one opponent card without changing orientations. Only a deck-drawn card discarded this turn activates powers. Powers are optional. Peek/spy lasts until explicit Hide, not a timer. The active player alone receives the value. Public actions expose target slots but not privately seen values.

Calling consumes your entire turn. Each OTHER player takes exactly one final turn. The caller's cards are still targetable. No snap, slapping, caller immunity, king -1, or standard-suit rules. Round ends after the final turn, or deck depletion. The caller scores zero only when lowest or tied lowest; otherwise sum +10. Everyone else scores their sum. Exactly 12,12,13,13 and no extras is Kamikaze: zero for that player, 50 for all others, overriding caller scoring.

Standard match: end when a total exceeds 100; exactly 100 resets to 50 once per player. Lowest total wins, tie by final round score, unresolved tie shared. Quick match: exactly four rounds, using the same hand scoring but no 100-to-50 reset and no early end above 100. This is the publisher's suggested short-session variant and the client default; Standard 100 is selectable in the lobby. No arbitrary turn cap is in the engine; the bot chooses to call by turn 22 to guarantee simulation progress.

Working brand: **CABO — Pocket Island** (the analyst’s recommended, uncleared working title). CABO-family compatibility is descriptive in help, not a claim of official affiliation. Rules prose and all character/environment visuals are original. Publisher PDFs are research evidence only and never served to the client. No Nintendo IP or audio recordings used. Brand clearance remains unverified; do not publish commercially without checking it.
